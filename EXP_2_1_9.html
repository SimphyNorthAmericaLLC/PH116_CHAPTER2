Chapter knowledge check multiple choice

A wave u = 5 cos(2x − 6t) satisfies the wave equation. What is its phase speed, and in which direction does it travel?

Correct approach: read k = 2 and ω = 6 directly from the argument (2x − 6t). Phase speed v = ω/k = 6/2 = 3 m/s. The minus sign confirms rightward travel. Verify: ∂²u/∂x² = −4u, ∂²u/∂t² = −36u → −4 = (1/9)(−36) → c = 3 ✓
